# Voxa Roadmap

This is product direction, not a promise of dates.

## Shipped

- realistic conversation scenarios
- Business English, Spanish, and Mandarin paths
- text-first practice
- AI replies and gentle corrections
- voice playback
- XP, streaks, stages, progress, and history
- optional account-backed sync
- App Store release
- provider diagnostics and release tooling

## Improving

- reliability and observability
- AI-provider fallbacks and cost control
- premium / RevenueCat completion
- accessibility
- App Store metadata and screenshot consistency
- speaking-session continuity and recovery

## Exploring

- deeper live voice practice
- pronunciation and fluency feedback
- broader language paths
- richer personalized missions
